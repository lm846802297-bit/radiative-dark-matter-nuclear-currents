# Three-body kinematics

The external process is

\[
\chi_1(p_\chi)+A(P_A)\to\chi_1(p_\chi')+A(P_A')+s(k_s).
\]

At fixed nuclear recoil \(E_R\),

\[
q_A=P_A'-P_A,\quad Q_A^2=2m_AE_R+E_R^2,\quad t_A=-2m_AE_R.
\]

Momentum conservation gives \(p_\chi-p_\chi'=q_A+k_s\). The invariant mass of the unobserved subsystem is \(W_{\chi s}^2=(p_\chi'+k_s)^2\). The two intermediate dark-state momenta are \(p_\chi-k_s\) and \(p_\chi'+k_s\).

Do not use the elastic two-body identity \(q_\chi=q_A\). Do not put \(k_s\) into the AFH Fourier transfer.
