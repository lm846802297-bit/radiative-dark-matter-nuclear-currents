# Calculation Result Archival Policy

Follow the central [Calculation-result archival policy](https://github.com/lm846802297-bit/high-energy-theory-research/blob/db503f0eb579bdbcac702e44237a6610c34690e7/docs/CALCULATION_RESULT_ARCHIVAL_POLICY.md), pinned to `db503f0eb579bdbcac702e44237a6610c34690e7`.

Archive source, inputs, exact commands, environment, native outputs, hashes, and validation evidence in this research repository. Preserve native data and immutable historical records. Do not use an expiring Actions artifact or temporary workspace as the sole copy of a scientific result.
